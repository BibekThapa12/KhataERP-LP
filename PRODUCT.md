# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

KhataERP serves two primary audiences equally:

- Nepalese small and medium business owners and operators, including retailers, wholesalers, distributors, and service businesses managing daily transactions and operations.
- Accountants and bookkeepers who need structured, accounting-correct records, controls, and financial reporting.

## Product Purpose

KhataERP brings accounting, inventory, sales, purchases, cash and bank activity, cheque management, and reporting into one connected cloud ERP. It helps businesses record everyday work in approachable language while maintaining the accounting structure professionals expect.

Success means operators can run daily business workflows confidently and accounting professionals can rely on the resulting books and reports without maintaining disconnected systems.

## Positioning

KhataERP combines two equally important advantages:

- Nepal-first simplicity through Nepali B.S. dates and fiscal years, NPR formatting, PAN/VAT identity fields, VAT-ready workflows, and locally familiar accounting terminology.
- Connected business operations where transactions update parties, ledgers, stock, cash, bank, cheques, and reports from the same underlying records.

## Operating Context

Businesses use KhataERP for sales and purchase documents, returns, receipts, payments, income, expenses, journal entries, inventory tracking and valuation, customer and supplier balances, cheque follow-up and clearance, and financial and operational reporting.

The product supports both plain-language entry flows for daily operators and formal voucher and reporting workflows for accounting professionals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Production-ready web product with authentication and tenant-scoped company data.
- Supports invoices, purchases, returns, receipts, payments, contra, income, expenses, journals, parties, ledgers, inventory, cheque management, dashboards, and financial reports.
- Supports B.S. fiscal years, NPR amounts, PAN/VAT fields, draft/complete/cancel flows, printable reports, CSV exports, and portable backup/restore workflows.
- Inventory includes products and services, alternate units, reorder levels, and Weighted Average, FIFO, or LIFO valuation.
- Accounting entries and important business rules are validated by the backend.
- Sign-in and sign-up entry points route to `https://khataerp.xyz/login`.
- Public pricing, VAT treatment for subscriptions, legal-policy links, and final wording for security claims remain explicit content decisions unless approved separately.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: KhataERP, presented as “Khata” with the descriptor “ERP for Nepal” in the current identity.
- Voice: practical, confident, clear, and locally relevant; approachable for operators without weakening professional accounting credibility.
- Preserve the Nepal-first and connected-operations positioning as equal product commitments.
- Existing client claims and supplied logos must use approved client data only; do not invent customers, testimonials, certifications, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

1. Make daily business work understandable without sacrificing accounting correctness.
2. Keep operational and financial records connected so information is entered once and remains consistent everywhere.
3. Treat Nepal-specific workflows as core product behavior, not localization added later.
4. Serve operators and accounting professionals with equal respect and credibility.
5. Publish only verified customer, pricing, security, and compliance claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should remain keyboard accessible, responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. Plain-language workflows should remain available alongside formal accounting terminology.
